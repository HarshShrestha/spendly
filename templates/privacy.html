Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects minimal data to provide the service. This includes account information (email, password) and the financial transaction data you choose to enter into the application.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking features of the app, such as generating spending reports and maintaining your user profile.

We employ secure storage methods to protect your data. Your financial entries are stored in a private database associated with your user account and are not accessible to other users.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share, sell, or lease your personal or financial data to third-party marketers or external services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
